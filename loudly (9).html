[FAQ]
How long until we see results?

Most clients see a clear lift in reach and engagement within the first 30 to 60 days. Revenue impact usually follows around month three.

Which platforms do you work with?

Instagram, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, LinkedIn and Facebook. We recommend the mix after the discovery call.

Do you create the content or just manage it?

Both. Our team handles concepts, shooting, editing, copy and publishing, so you don't need to brief five different people.

What does a project cost?

Packages start at $1,800 per month. After the intro call you get a fixed proposal with no hidden fees.

Is there a minimum contract?

We work in 3-month cycles, because good social takes time to compound. After that you can continue monthly.
